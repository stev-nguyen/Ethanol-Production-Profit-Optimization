# Ethanol-Production-Profit-Optimization
Project Overview

This project focuses on business decision analysis and profit optimization for Eddie's Ethanol Co. using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to evaluate how ethanol selling prices and biomass procurement decisions affect production profitability. Through financial modeling and sensitivity analysis, the project explores different business scenarios to identify strategies that maximize expected monthly profit.

This project was completed collaboratively as part of BANA 4095 at the University of Cincinnati.

Business Problem

Eddie's Ethanol Co. must make important operational and financial decisions regarding ethanol pricing and biomass procurement.

Choosing an appropriate selling price and contract quantity can significantly influence production costs and overall profitability.

The project uses an Excel-based decision model to evaluate different combinations of business assumptions and identify profit-maximizing strategies.

Project Objectives

Develop an Excel-based financial decision model.

Analyze the relationship between ethanol selling prices and profitability.

Evaluate biomass procurement and contract quantity decisions.

Perform sensitivity analysis under different business scenarios.

Identify pricing and procurement strategies that maximize modeled monthly profit.

Results & Key Findings

1. Ethanol Pricing Analysis

With the biomass contract quantity fixed at 300 tons, the model identified an ethanol selling price of $2.29 per gallon as the profit-maximizing option among the tested prices.

Modeled average monthly profit: $54,295.38

2. Biomass Contract Quantity Analysis

With the ethanol selling price fixed at $2.50 per gallon, the model identified a contract quantity of 1,075 tons as the optimal tested quantity.

Modeled average monthly profit: $46,734.62

3. Final Pricing Recommendation

The final analysis identified an ethanol selling price of $2.27 per gallon as the profit-maximizing option under the final model assumptions.

Modeled maximum monthly profit: $57,212.31

These results illustrate how pricing and procurement decisions can influence overall business profitability.

Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important considerations:

Selling price decisions directly influence projected profitability.

Biomass procurement quantities affect production costs and operational performance.

Sensitivity analysis helps evaluate how changes in business assumptions affect financial outcomes.

Pricing and procurement decisions should be evaluated together rather than independently.
